Methodology

Gravity = catGate(Catalysis) × survGate(RobustSurvival) × shape(0.45·Design + 0.25·Breadth + 0.30·Indispensability). A measure of durable, load-bearing structure others build on — not commit count.

Gravity is a present-tense reading. It reflects the relationship at a moment in time; the same needle moves a founder from a near-maximum peak down to a few points as the live load moves elsewhere. A low figure today is not a permanent verdict.

Catalysis / RobustSurvival are the two gates. RobustSurvival counts only lines that endure in modules where other authors keep committing; Catalysis asks whether others built on the surviving foundation. If either is near zero, the product collapses — ownership or founding alone cannot mint Gravity.

Founders are the hardest test of a relational metric: an honest lens must credit them when their code load-bears and let them fade when it is superseded — without ever erasing that they were once central. Odersky's profile does exactly this. The founding peak is unmistakable; the present-day decline is not a demotion but a reading of where the live structure now rests.
